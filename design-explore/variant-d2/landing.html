The bond comes before the first play เงินประกันมาก่อนจังหวะแรก

A venue stakes 1,200 mTHB in VenueStake before it may sell a single play. Rooms without an active bond are refused at the protocol's door: HTTP 402 — payment required of the venue, never of the fan.

  • ✓ Fake plays are slashed to the protocol treasury.
  • ✓ Unbonded rooms sell nothing; the fan never sees a broken promise.
