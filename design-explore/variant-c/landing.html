CH 02

Venues post a bond before they can sell a play

ร้านต้องฝากพันธบัตรก่อนจึงจะขายการเล่นเพลงได้

The VenueStake contract holds a 1,200 mTHB bond per venue. No bond on chain, no play sales — the protocol refuses unbonded sellers at the contract level, before a fan ever pays.

Fake or unverifiable plays get slashed: part of the bond burns to the protocol treasury, so pushing junk plays costs the venue real money, not a warning letter.

mTHB is a demo stand-in for a BOT-licensed THB stable targeted at the Bank of Thailand March-2027 framework.
