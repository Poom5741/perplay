How a play settlesการเล่นหนึ่งครั้งชำระอย่างไร

  1. 01

    Scan at the table

    The fan scans the QR on the table card. The request carries the track, the venue, and the price: ฿20 to ฿50 per play.

  2. 02

    Pay in mTHB demo token

    The payment moves from the fan's wallet into the play escrow. Mock THB stands in for the BOT-licensed THB stable targeted at the Bank of Thailand March-2027 framework.

  3. 03

    Verify the play happened

    Live band nights match the venue's setlist. DJ nights match an audio fingerprint. Either way, the venue confirms the song actually played.

  4. 04

    Split and anchor

    70% label, 10% venue, 20% platform settle on BSC Testnet (chain 97), and the receipt is anchored as an immutable on-chain record.

Venues post a bond before they can sellสถานที่ต้องวางพันธบัตรก่อนขายเพลย์

Before a venue can sell a single play, it stakes 1,200 mTHB demo token in the VenueStake contract. Unbonded venues are refused at the protocol level, so the gate is the contract, not a promise.

When a fake play is proven, the stake is slashed to the protocol treasury. The money funds the protocol, not the reporter. In this demo the slash authority is the admin account; production runs it behind a multisig.
